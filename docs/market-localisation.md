# Market Localisation Hypotheses

This document records product hypotheses for discussion and portfolio purposes. It is not a claim that one market needs a single universal solution.

## Shared product layer

Across markets, the reusable core could include:

- consolidated financial-health view;
- financial-language simplification;
- prioritised next-best actions;
- consent and source traceability;
- AI/deterministic-system boundaries;
- configurable rules by jurisdiction and product type.

## China hypothesis

A China-oriented version would likely focus less on basic mobile-payment access and more on financial fragmentation and comprehension, for example:

- multi-provider financial overview;
- cross-insurer policy organisation;
- financial-health and liquidity analysis;
- ageing/accessibility considerations;
- fraud and scam warnings;
- strong consent and sensitive-data controls;
- explainable AI guidance rather than autonomous execution.

## Southeast Asia hypothesis

Priorities can vary significantly by market, but product extensions may include:

- remittance and cross-border money movement;
- accessible insurance / protection journeys;
- wallet-first experiences;
- financial-inclusion use cases;
- Takaful / Shariah considerations where relevant;
- low-bandwidth and lightweight mobile journeys;
- multi-currency needs.

## Product principle

**Same platform, different user problem.**

Localisation should begin with the local customer problem, payment and banking rails, regulatory constraints, language, trust model and distribution channel rather than simply translating a global interface.
