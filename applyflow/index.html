ApplyFlow AI

An AI application-operations assistant that turns fragmented applicant status, missing documents and deadlines into prioritised next actions. / 一个把学生状态、材料缺口与截止日期整理成“下一步该做什么”的 AI 申请运营助手。

1. The problem / 问题

Application operations are fragmented across messaging apps, spreadsheets, documents, email, calendars and university portals. The real risk is not “can we write another document?” but “what needs to happen next, for whom, by when, and has anything been missed?”

Fragmented status

Applicant data and document progress live in different tools.

Deadline risk

Urgency changes daily, but static spreadsheets do not prioritise work automatically.

Manual follow-up

Consultants repeatedly check, remind, assign and re-check the same workflow.

2. Product idea / 产品思路

ApplyFlow creates one operational layer across student records, document checklists, deadlines and tasks. AI does not make admissions decisions; it identifies operational risk, explains why a case is urgent and recommends the next best action.

Read status→Detect missing items→Compare deadline→Prioritise risk→Recommend action→Create follow-up task
Design principle

AI should reduce coordination work, not invent applicant facts. Material status comes from structured records; AI interprets, prioritises and assists execution.

3. What the prototype demonstrates / 原型展示什么

Control center

Sorts applicants by deadline risk and document completeness instead of by name.

Ask AI

Natural-language query such as “Which applicants have deadlines within 14 days but missing documents?”

Next best action

Suggests reminders, document requests and follow-up tasks with an explicit reason.
