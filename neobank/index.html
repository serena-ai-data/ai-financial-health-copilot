01
User tension

Good financial products can still create a fragmented financial life.

Users may already have capable banking, insurance and investment apps. The unmet need appears between products: understanding the whole position, knowing what deserves attention, and deciding what to do next.

“I do not need another dashboard. I need one reliable answer to: what deserves attention now?”
Primary user hypothesis · working professionals using multiple digital financial products
01

Financial context is split across providers

Balances, cards, investments and policies are individually visible but difficult to interpret together.

02

Users carry the integration burden

They manually combine product terms, risk signals, spending context and coverage information before making a decision.

03

Feature menus do not equal prioritisation

A product can offer many capabilities while still failing to answer what the user should review first.

China-first visual language

Local familiarity without copying an existing bank.

The red-led palette is a deliberate localisation hypothesis rather than decoration. Red carries familiar positive and high-salience associations in China and appears across parts of Chinese financial branding. The interface pairs it with warm ivory and restrained gold so the product feels locally recognisable, trustworthy and premium without becoming festive or overly promotional.

Deep red

Creates immediate salience and a familiar China-market financial cue while reserving the strongest colour for priority states and decisive actions.

Warm ivory

Softens the intensity of red, improves reading comfort and keeps dense financial information calm rather than sales-led.

Restrained gold

Adds a premium accent sparingly for hierarchy and emphasis without turning the interface into an ornamental or festive visual system.

Design principle: locally recognisable, not imitative. Colour supports trust and product hierarchy; it does not substitute for user research, usability testing or evidence about individual preferences.
02
Product thesis

Build one reusable decision layer across financial services.

The launch thesis is deliberately narrower than “build every neobank feature.” First prove that users value a trusted cross-product decision layer; expand transaction and product breadth only after that value is visible.

I

Context

Bring bank, card, insurance, investment and user-confirmed facts into one inspectable financial state.

II

Decision

Use deterministic calculations, visible uncertainty and bounded AI explanation to rank what matters.

III

Action

Move into planning, comparison or regulated execution only after explicit user review and confirmation.

Core journey
Consent→Financial context→Unified state→Priority signal→AI explanation→Human-confirmed action

04
Launch plan

A 90-day path from product hypothesis to measurable learning.

The objective is not to ship the broadest app. It is to prove one valuable cross-product journey, measure behaviour after launch, and use production signals to decide what deserves expansion.

0
Days 0–30

Validate the wedge

Test onboarding, manual/context input, unified money state, explainability and priority action. Instrument drop-off, correction and trust signals before adding breadth.

30
Days 31–60

Launch a narrow production flow

Release the strongest user journey to a controlled cohort, monitor product/AI issues, define escalation rules and run weekly prioritisation using production evidence.

60
Days 61–90

Expand only where behaviour supports it

Prioritise connectors, savings routines, protection actions or other services based on repeat usage, action engagement, trust failures and operational cost.

Experiment 01

Dashboard vs priority-first home

Test whether leading with the single highest-priority action improves action engagement without reducing trust.

Experiment 02

Manual context vs progressive connection

Test whether a lighter manual MVP reaches useful insight faster than requesting many integrations upfront.

Experiment 03

Explanation depth

Compare concise reasoning with expanded formulas/source details to understand when transparency helps or overwhelms.

06
Trust & AI controls

AI can make finance clearer without becoming the hidden source of truth.

The product separates verified financial state, deterministic calculations and AI-generated explanation so users and operators can inspect what changed and why.

Facts

Verified state stays explicit.

Owned by deterministic systems: balances, confirmed inputs, score calculations, transaction state, threshold logic.

AI

Interpretation remains bounded.

AI may: explain, summarise, surface uncertainty and answer grounded questions about verified product state.

Action

Consequential changes require control.

Human confirmation: required before transactions, product hand-off or other higher-risk execution.

Release-blocking behaviour

AI changes verified numbers, invents missing financial facts, hides material uncertainty or encourages execution beyond the product’s allowed boundary.

07
Execution evidence

Separate what is built from what is proposed.

This case intentionally distinguishes demonstrable product artefacts from future roadmap hypotheses.

What reviewers can inspect today

The goal is to demonstrate product thinking through working artefacts, not imply that a concept portfolio has already shipped as a regulated neobank.
