AI Insights

Explain the decision, not just the recommendation

Bounded AI layer
AI may explain verified results and surface uncertainty. It must not silently alter balances, verified facts, score calculations or transaction state.
Priority

Why check the card balance first?

A short-term balance may be harmless if it is non-interest-bearing or already scheduled for repayment. If it carries high interest, it can materially change the priority of increasing investment exposure.

Grounded in: verified balance ¥12,000 + unknown interest status + current liquidity. Missing context remains explicitly unknown.
Uncertainty

What the product refuses to assume

It does not infer card APR, repayment schedule, investment suitability or product eligibility when those facts are unavailable.

Boundary: explain uncertainty → request confirmation → recalculate from verified state.
