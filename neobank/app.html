Priority

Why is debt review ranked first?

Because a ¥12,000 balance exists, but the product does not know whether it is interest-bearing, overdue or within an interest-free period.

Grounding: verified card balance + missing interest-status field. The AI can explain the missing context; it cannot silently classify the debt as high-cost.
Liquidity

Do I have enough emergency cash?

The current cash reserve covers about 5.3 months of essential expenses in this synthetic profile.

Grounding: ¥42,000 cash reserve ÷ ¥8,000 essential monthly expenses. The calculation remains deterministic.

Human confirmation by design

Verify card interest status

Confirm whether the current balance is interest-bearing before changing repayment or investment priorities.

Why confirmation comes first

Debt cost is a material decision input. The product should not infer it from balance alone. A production flow could request statement data or ask the user to confirm the status before giving more specific guidance.

Review protection uncertainty

One policy field is incomplete. Verify the source before using it in protection-gap guidance.

Source-first verification

The product would show the policy source, extracted field and confidence state, allowing the user to correct the information before it influences recommendations.

Build a stronger emergency reserve

If the user confirms a target above the current 5.3 months, the app can translate the gap into a monthly savings plan.
